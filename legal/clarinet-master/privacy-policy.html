Clarinet Master — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clarinet Master is a practice companion for clarinet players, available for Android (Google Play) and for iPhone and iPad (App Store). This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and how it is handled. Where the two versions differ, it says so.

In short

  • Your playing is analysed on your device. Audio is never uploaded.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your practice history and recordings stay on your device.
  • The iPhone and iPad version contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The Android version uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (anonymous usage and crash data, see below).
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Microphone

Clarinet Master uses the microphone to detect the pitch of the notes you play while a practice screen is open (tuner and drone, drills, sheet music, the live fingering chart, call and response, microphone calibration and recording). The sound is processed in real time, in memory, on your device. No audio is uploaded, transmitted or stored on any server. The microphone is only used while one of those screens is open.

You can withdraw microphone access at any time: on iPhone and iPad in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, on Android in Settings → Apps → Clarinet Master → Permissions. The practice screens then ask for it again; the rest of the app keeps working.

Recordings

When you record yourself (Recording screen, or a take saved while playing along in Sheet music), a WAV file is saved in the app's private storage on your device. Recordings never leave your device unless you choose to share one through the system share sheet. You can rename or delete them in the app, and delete them in bulk in Settings → Storage. On iPhone and iPad they are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) like other app data, if you have backups turned on; that backup is managed by Apple, not by us.

Practice history and settings

Exercise scores, note accuracy, practice time, streaks, badges, Learning Journey progress, your settings and any MusicXML score you import are stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences, iOS app storage). They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Usage and crash data

iPhone and iPad: the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK and makes no network requests of its own. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics; this is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings.

Android: the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to receive automatic crash reports. This data is anonymous: it does not include your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or recordings. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Clarinet Master Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. On iPhone and iPad it is processed by Apple through the App Store; on Android by Google Play Billing. We never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether Pro has been purchased with your Apple Account or Google account, so it can unlock the Pro content; "Restore Purchases" asks the store again.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily practice reminder (Settings → Reminders), your device asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device; no push server is involved. You can turn it off in the same place, or in the system notification settings.

Contacting us

If you send feedback from the app, your mail app opens a message to us; we receive what you choose to send (your email address and your message) and use it only to answer you.

What we do NOT collect

  • Audio of your playing, other than recordings you make yourself, which stay on your device.
  • Your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us).
  • Your location, contacts, photos, calendar or any other data on your device.
  • Any advertising or tracking identifier. The app does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Clarinet Master collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and contains no advertising, chat or user-generated content.

Changes to this policy
