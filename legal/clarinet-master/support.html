Clarinet Master — Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to frederic.valente@gmail.com (or use Settings → Send feedback in the app). Please mention your device and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

The app does not hear me

Clarinet Master needs the microphone. On iPhone and iPad, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Clarinet Master. Play a steady note close to the device; in a noisy room, run Library → Mic calibration.

The tuner shows a different note from my piano

Notes are shown as written for your clarinet. On a B♭ clarinet, written C sounds as concert B♭, so the app shows a note one whole tone above the piano. Choose your clarinet (B♭, A, E♭, bass, basset horn) and your reference A (440, 442 or 443 Hz) in Settings → Instrument.

Restore my purchase

Clarinet Master Pro is a one-time purchase linked to your Apple Account. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

What is free

Everything is unlocked for your first 3 days. After that, the tuner, metronome, fingering charts, Learning Journey, scale drills and 5 sheet-music pieces stay free. Clarinet Master Pro unlocks every drill type, the full sheet-music library, your personal tendency chart, unlimited recordings and every clarinet type. No subscription.

My recordings and data

Everything stays on your device: no account, nothing uploaded. Recordings can be shared, renamed or deleted from the Recording screen, and cleared in Settings → Storage. Deleting the app deletes all its data. See the privacy policy.
