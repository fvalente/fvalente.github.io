Trumpet Master — Support

Trumpet Master listens as you play and shows, in real time, which notes you hit and how in tune you are, with a guided Learning Journey, 30 lessons, a tuner with drone, a metronome and backing tracks.

Need help or found a bug?
Email frederic.valente@gmail.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app (it adds your app and iOS version automatically). We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

The app doesn't hear my notes

Check that the microphone is allowed: iPhone Settings → Apps → Trumpet Master → Microphone. In a noisy room, open Utilities → Microphone calibration and set the silence threshold so background noise stays below the line. Headphones help if you play along with the drone, the metronome or a backing track.

My notes show as the wrong pitch

Choose your instrument (B♭, C, D, E♭, piccolo or bass trumpet) in Settings: notes are shown at written pitch for that instrument. You can also change the A4 reference (default 440 Hz) and the temperament there.

What is free, and what does Pro unlock?

Everything is unlimited for the first 3 days. After that, Practice and the tuner/drone are free for 20 minutes a day, every day; lessons and the metronome are never limited. Trumpet Master Pro is a single in-app purchase (no subscription, no ads) that removes the daily limit, keeps all your recordings and unlocks every backing-track key.

I bought Pro on another iPhone, or after reinstalling

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my playing recorded or uploaded?

No. Audio is analysed on your device and never leaves it. Recordings you make on purpose are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
