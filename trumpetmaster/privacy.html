Trumpet Master for iPhone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Trumpet Master is a practice companion for trumpet players. This policy explains what the iPhone app accesses, why, and what happens to it. The short version: the app does not collect any data about you, and nothing you play ever leaves your device.

Microphone

Trumpet Master uses your iPhone's microphone to detect the pitch of the notes you play in the practice, tuner/drone and calibration screens. Audio is analysed on your device in real time and is never uploaded, shared or sent anywhere. The microphone is only active while one of those screens is listening.

If you use the Recordings feature, the audio you choose to record is saved as a file inside the app on your device. It is never uploaded. You can delete recordings in the app at any time, and they are removed if you delete the app.

Practice history and settings

Your exercise scores, streaks, Learning Journey progress, daily practice minutes and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

No analytics, no tracking, no account

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the iPhone app.
  • No advertising and no tracking of any kind, across apps or websites.
  • No account or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • The app works without an internet connection.

Purchases

Trumpet Master Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro is owned on your Apple Account, so it can remove the free-tier limits. "Restore Purchases" in Settings asks Apple to confirm that ownership again.

Feedback emails

If you choose "Send feedback" in Settings, your Mail app opens a draft that includes the app version, iOS version, device model and language, so we can reproduce problems. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you send only to answer you and improve the app.

Children

Trumpet Master is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
